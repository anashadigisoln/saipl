Pioneering the Future of PP/BOPP/HDPE & Leno Fabrics

At SAIPL, we engineer next-generation flexible packaging and industrial fabric solutions designed to meet the rigorous demands of global supply chains. By integrating high-precision extrusion, automated weaving technology, and state-of-the-art multi-layer lamination, we deliver durable, weather-resistant, and sustainable polymer composites across industries.

From high-tensile PP woven fabrics to high-definition BOPP laminated bags and ventilated Leno mesh bags for agricultural use, our products ensure structural integrity, zero leakage, and maximum load optimization.
